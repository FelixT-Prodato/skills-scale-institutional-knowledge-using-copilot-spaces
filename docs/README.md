## Overview

OctoAcme's project management processes are built upon clear principles that prioritize customer value, iterative delivery, data-driven decisions, and well-defined ownership. Every project is guided by a concise lifecycle: initiation, planning, execution, release, and retrospective. This workflow ensures that business needs are validated early through artifacts like a Project One-pager, stakeholder lists, and risk registers, allowing the team to align on goals, timelines, and responsibilities before detailed planning begins. During planning, work is broken down into actionable items with clear acceptance criteria, estimated effort, and a defined Definition of Done, all managed within organized backlogs and project boards.

Distinct roles are central to the OctoAcme approach. The Project Manager (PM) is tasked with orchestrating delivery, managing schedules, and fostering communication. The Product Manager (PdM) defines the product vision, priorities, and success metrics. Developers focus on designing, building, and testing software, collaborating closely with product and project leads. QA/testing ensures released features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards. Cross-functional stakeholders are engaged throughout, providing input and approvals at key stages.

Communication is embedded at multiple levels, from daily standups and weekly delivery syncs to monthly stakeholder updates. Project managers facilitate regular status checks, risk discussions, and retrospectives, ensuring transparency and rapid issue escalation when needed. Artifacts such as risk registers, decision logs, and weekly status reports keep everyone informed, while project boards visualize progress from backlog to delivery.

Quality assurance practices are rigorous, with automated testing, code review, and CI linting required before merging work. End-to-end smoke testing and manual QA are emphasized for critical flows, particularly before releases. Security scanning and rollback plans are built into the release workflow. After each milestone, retrospectives identify improvements and assign actionable follow-ups, establishing a culture of continuous learning and process refinement that keeps the team aligned and adaptive.
